Getting around +

Slide to unlock when you open your iPhone. Tap the screen to open an app. Drag to scroll, and use the round Home button to return. The buttons along the edge control sleep, volume, and silent mode.

Save keeps a checkpoint in this browser. Export downloads a backup; Import restores one. Reset starts again from the supplied phone state.

The current starting phone fixes the long pause when returning Home and has about 6 MB of free storage. Existing saved phones keep their old configuration. Export a backup before Reset to try the updated starting phone.

Keep offline downloads the phone for later visits without a connection. Browser storage can be cleared, so export a backup of anything you want to keep.

This is genuine iPhone OS 1.0 running in an emulator. Hardware and app support are still being developed. Internet access, cellular service, Camera, and audio are not yet available.
