The original 2007 iPhone, in your browser

2007.mobile is an interactive emulator of the first-generation iPhone. It runs genuine iPhone OS 1.0, build 1A543a, with the original lock screen, Home screen and applications.

Try the classic iPhone experience

Open the emulator, wait for the phone to load, and slide to unlock. Tap an app to open it, drag to scroll, and press the round Home button to return. Explore Settings, try a calculation in Calculator, or look around Notes, Contacts and Calendar.

On a keyboard, Escape or H presses Home, P controls sleep and wake, + and − adjust volume, and M toggles silent mode.

Real software on emulated hardware

The app screens are drawn by Apple's original software. They are not recreated as web pages. A WebAssembly emulator models the original iPhone's ARM processor and S5L8900 hardware; HTML and CSS provide the surrounding phone case and controls.

The emulated device is an iPhone1,1, also known as M68AP. Its screen retains the original 320 × 480 resolution. The project builds on S5LBox research and adds an original-iPhone machine target and browser runtime.

What works, and what is still in progress

Unlocking, touch input, Home navigation and several original apps work. Hardware and app support are still being developed. Internet access inside the phone, cellular service, Camera and audio are not yet available. This is an emulator for exploring the original experience, not a replacement for a physical phone.

The emulator stores phone checkpoints in this browser. Browser storage can be cleared, so it should not be treated as permanent storage.

Explore the project

The source code and engineering notes describe the hardware model, working features and current limitations. 2007.mobile is an independent project and is not affiliated with Apple.
